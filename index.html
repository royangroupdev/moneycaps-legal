Moneycaps — Privacy Policy

Effective 22 July 2026

What we collect

  • Account info — your name, email, and phone number, stored with our backend provider (Supabase) to run your login.
  • Financial data — transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, and notes you enter. These stay encrypted on your own device and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Receipt photos — encrypted and stored on your device only. If you use AI receipt scanning, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini API solely to read the amount/merchant/date off it.
  • Subscription status — handled by RevenueCat and Apple/Google, so we never see your payment card details.
  • Referral code — if you share or use one, we record which account referred which, to pay out affiliate rewards.

What we don't do

  • We never see or ask for your bank login, card number, or OTPs — Moneycaps is manual entry and receipt scanning only.
  • We don't run analytics or ad tracking, and we don't sell your data to anyone.
  • Moneycaps is an independent budgeting app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any bank.

Security

Your transactions and receipts are encrypted on-device (AES-256) before they're saved. Account traffic to our backend runs over HTTPS. You can also turn on Face ID/Touch ID app lock in this device's settings.

Your rights

You can edit your profile any time in Settings, and permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Delete account. For any other request (data export, correction), contact us at support@moneycaps.app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
